# Risk register

| ID | Risk | Likelihood / impact | Early signal | Mitigation and fallback | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R-001 | Hardware arrives too late for full validation. | High / critical | No delivery or inventory by Sept. 5. | Finish all simulator/software/docs first; prepare exact bring-up checklists; use recorded replay only as a clearly labeled fallback. | Open |
| R-002 | Broad pretrained audio model performs poorly on selected real events. | High / high | Weak precision/recall or calibration in licensed desktop evaluation. | Limit vocabulary, tune class mappings/thresholds, temporal confirmation, and demonstrate one excellent repeatable scenario. | Open |
| R-003 | UDP loss/jitter creates audio gaps or high latency. | Medium / high | Sequence gaps, underflows, or p95 latency above target. | 20 ms frames, bounded jitter buffer, silence/hold policy, health metrics; consider redundancy/compression only after measurements. | Open |
| R-004 | XIAO microphone or ESP32 core API differs from documentation. | Medium / high | Constant samples, compile failure, or distorted capture. | Pin Arduino-ESP32 3.x, use `ESP_I2S` PDM pins GPIO42/GPIO41, retain official 2.x alternative notes, and run mic smoke test first. | Open |
| R-005 | Battery pigtail polarity is reversed relative to board/cell expectations. | Medium / critical | Red/black or connector polarity mismatch. | Never connect by color alone; require disconnected continuity/polarity checks with multimeter; no battery during soldering. | Open |
| R-006 | 500 mAh runtime is inadequate under continuous Wi-Fi/audio. | Medium / medium | Measured current implies short demo runtime or voltage sag. | USB development mode, duty-cycle experiments, lower Wi-Fi power only after reliability; V2 battery redesign. | Open |
| R-007 | App Lab/Bridge versions change or are unstable. | Medium / high | Official examples or app manifest fail on installed version. | Pin/document tested versions, keep standalone core, use thin App Lab adapter, preserve Arduino CLI MCU build path. | Open |
| R-008 | Autodesk rules page placeholders cause ambiguous eligibility. | High / medium | Official rules retain generic template fields. | Follow specific overview and organizer guide; save checked URLs/dates; include UNO Q, Fusion `.f3d`, BOM, PCBWay plan, project story, and all application questions. | Mitigated |
| R-009 | Two contest entries appear identical or reuse a winning entry. | Medium / critical | Shared story/CAD/interactions dominate V2. | Separate names, stories, CAD, interactions, portability, manufacturing, testing, and shared-work ledger; never submit an already-winning identical entry. | Open |
| R-010 | Private/copyrighted audio or third-party assets enter the submission. | Medium / critical | Missing provenance or accidental recording in Git. | Ignore recordings/datasets; use manifests and owned/compatible assets; review release archive licenses. | Mitigated |
| R-011 | Dashboard looks polished but is not immediately legible on video. | Medium / medium | Small text, poor contrast, unclear active event. | High-contrast accessible tokens, dominant cue card, responsive layout, mock video capture review. | Open |
| R-012 | CAD dimensions do not match shipped parts. | High / high | Enclosure interference on first fit. | Central parameter table, conservative clearance, serviceable fasteners, and physical dimension validation before manufacturing. | Open |
| R-013 | Product is mistaken for a certified smoke/baby/security monitor. | Medium / critical | Copy implies guaranteed detection. | Persistent awareness-aid limitation in UI, docs, narration, and submissions; test but do not guarantee safety classes. | Mitigated |
| R-014 | Private GitHub link is inaccessible to judges. | Medium / high | Hackster preview logged out cannot open source. | Prepare a sanitized public release/archive or attach source at submission; verify from logged-out browser. | Open; user decision later |
