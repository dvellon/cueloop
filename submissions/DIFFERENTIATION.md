# Competition differentiation ledger

CueLoop V1 and CueLoop Bridge V2 share an original technical foundation but are separate products and submissions. This ledger prevents the Autodesk entry from becoming a resubmission of the App Lab prototype.

| Area | CueLoop V1 — App Lab | CueLoop Bridge V2 — Autodesk University 2027 |
|---|---|---|
| Primary outcome | Prove a private, uncertainty-aware remote acoustic event pipeline for social impact. | Deliver a compact, carry-on-safe, manufacturable multi-space accessory for creators and Autodesk customers. |
| Primary user story | A person in another room notices one selected household/workspace event. | A designer, maker, technician, or attendee carries a receiver while monitoring a fabrication process, door, timer, or collaborator. |
| Hardware scope | UNO Q receiver/prototype, one XIAO Sense CuePod, minimum reliable available output. | Dockable CuePod, portable receiver, clip/stand/lanyard system, explicit visual+haptic language, charging/power architecture, potential carrier PCB. |
| Interaction | Dashboard plus acknowledge/mute and one physical cue. | Eyes-free cue language, location/pod identity, tactile controls, carry/dock state, creator-focused modes, multi-pod path. |
| Enclosure | Reproducible prototype protection and mounting. | Serviceable production concept with microphone labyrinth, battery restraint, acoustic path, DFM, assembly time, end-of-line test, and PCBWay process. |
| Fusion work | Reference enclosures and dimensions may be shared as prior foundation. | New master assembly, parameterized attachment system, electronics layout, Fusion Electronics carrier, DFM iterations, and manufacturing package. |
| Testing | Accuracy, false alerts, loss, latency, privacy, and recovery for one pod/demo. | Adds wear/carry ergonomics, drop/clip/dock cycles, multi-space creator workflows, charge/runtime, acoustic port orientation, assembly and manufacturing tests. |
| Submission story | Accessible local-first awareness and reproducible App Lab contribution. | A product Autodesk users would carry; manufacturability at approximately 750 units and value across studios, shops, travel, and AU. |

## Shared work allowed

- Versioned acoustic transport concepts, event vocabulary research, uncertainty engine principles, privacy model, and lessons from V1 testing.
- Original code may be evolved with attribution to the same author and an explicit change log.
- Measurements may be cited as prior prototype evidence only when labeled with the exact V1 configuration.

## V2-only evidence required before final submission

- New Fusion `.f3d` master assembly and Electronics design.
- PCBWay-manufactured enclosure and its DFM/fit results.
- Portable receiver and dockable-pod physical interaction.
- Haptic/visual cue usability and Autodesk-user workflow tests.
- V2 BOM, schematics, power results, photos, video, and build article.
- A public paragraph explaining what was inherited and what is materially new.
