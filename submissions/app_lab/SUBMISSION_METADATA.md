# App Lab submission metadata

## Recommended title

**CueLoop: Private Room-to-Room Sound Awareness with UNO Q Edge AI**

## Short title

**CueLoop**

## Subtitle / short description

**A wireless microphone pod turns selected nearby sounds into uncertainty-aware visual cues—locally, without cloud audio or default recordings.**

## Category strategy

Primary: **Best Social Impact**. CueLoop begins with access for people who are deaf or hard of hearing, then broadens to headphone users, people wearing hearing protection, caregivers, neurodivergent users, and makers monitoring another space. Best in Show is upside, not a different claim.

## One-sentence pitch

CueLoop uses a detachable XIAO microphone pod and both processors of Arduino UNO Q to recognize a small set of important sounds locally, wait for temporal confirmation, and translate them into discreet, understandable cues.
