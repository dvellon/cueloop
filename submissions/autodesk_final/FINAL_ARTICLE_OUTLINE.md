# CueLoop Bridge final Hackster article outline

This outline reserves a complete December story while keeping all unobserved fields explicitly gated. Write the final prose from evidence rather than filling brackets with aspiration.

## Recommended title and opening

**CueLoop Bridge: Carry Awareness, Not Recordings**

Open with one observed Autodesk-user moment: the creator leaves a printer, machine, test, cure, or studio event but carries a calm local cue. Show the finished portable receiver and placed CuePod in the first image/video seconds.

## Final article sequence

1. **The work should not own the room** — observed workflow/person/problem, baseline workaround, and why a narrower local acoustic bridge helps.
2. **From CueLoop to CueLoop Bridge** — concise lineage plus a visual table proving new receiver portability, dock, interaction, power, CAD/electronics, manufacturing, testing, and workflow.
3. **What the finished product does** — placement, selection, observing/confirming, physical cue, acknowledge/mute, carry/dock/service; limitation as awareness aid.
4. **Why UNO Q is necessary** — measured Linux inference/UI/network role, STM32 deterministic cue/input/health role, Bridge boundary, recovery evidence.
5. **Privacy and uncertainty are physical interactions** — volatile audio, metadata controls, trusted-LAN limitation, confirmation comparison, visible health/fault states.
6. **System architecture** — power, signal, protocol, processor ownership, portable output, dock/pod relationship, threat/safety boundaries.
7. **Exact BOM** — minimum delivered product versus tools/test equipment; manufacturer/part/qty/function/source/date/currency/actual cost; alternates and unknowns.
8. **Electronics and power** — full schematic/netlist, load drivers/protection, power path/charging, pin budget, worst case and measured current/thermal/runtime.
9. **Fusion mechanical design** — measured parameters, parts/joints, acoustic path, antenna/thermal keepouts, clip/stand/dock, cell restraint, service order, interference and drawings.
10. **Fusion Electronics/carrier decision** — actual linked design/build benefit or evidence-backed explanation for no carrier.
11. **PCBWay DFM and iterations** — coupon/process/material/finish, released files, supplier feedback, received inspection, fit failures/revisions, manufacturability plan.
12. **Firmware, App Lab, and source** — pinned/reproducible build, model manifest, protocol, simulator, tests, contribution/reuse paths.
13. **Build it safely** — exact electrical/mechanical assembly, polarity/continuity, current-limited/USB-first power, provisioning and no-secret workflow.
14. **Use it in the creator workflow** — one uninterrupted observed task, ambiguous background case, fault/recovery, local/no-audio proof.
15. **Results** — per-class and policy comparison, inference/end-to-end latency, packet/RF/recovery, portable power/thermal, acoustic enclosure, attachment cycles/load/drop, usability and assembly time; every row labeled.
16. **What failed and changed** — dropped classes/features, CAD/manufacturing iterations, false alerts, power/mechanical constraints.
17. **Approximately 750 units** — assembly/EOL, test fixtures, yield/inspection, cost/quote/lifecycle/regulatory work still required.
18. **Limits and next work** — no certification/safety replacement, hostile-network security gap, dataset/user-study bounds, commercial/manufacturing gates.
19. **Credits, source, and reproducibility** — licenses, third-party model/dependencies, native `.f3d`, electronics, firmware/App, test manifests, stable public downloads.
