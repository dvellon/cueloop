# CueLoop Bridge Autodesk final submission checklist

**Official deadline currently recorded:** December 20, 2026 at 11:59 PM PST / December 21 at 2:59 AM EST. Recheck the live overview, rules, FAQ, and organizer instructions before freezing.

## Eligibility and differentiation

- [ ] Entry remains original, eligible, and not an identical already-winning project.
- [ ] `submissions/DIFFERENTIATION.md` matches the final build and public copy.
- [ ] New V2 hardware, interaction, native CAD, manufacturing, tests, workflow, photos, and video are visible—not merely promised.
- [ ] Shared V1 code/results are identified with exact commit/configuration and do not imply V2 physical validation.

## Product and evidence

- [ ] Portable/attachable/carryable behavior is physically demonstrated and useful beyond Autodesk University.
- [ ] One real Autodesk-creator workflow is observed, documented, and understandable without narration.
- [ ] UNO Q and both processors perform essential measured roles.
- [ ] Privacy, uncertainty, health/fault, acknowledgement, and non-color-only cue behavior are demonstrated.
- [ ] `EVIDENCE_MATRIX.md` has no unsupported final claim; failures and limits remain visible.

## Fusion, PCBWay, electronics, and manufacturing

- [ ] Attach the final native `.f3d`, measured parameter/design record, drawings, assembly, and relevant neutral/manufacturing exports.
- [ ] Attach complete readable schematic/netlist and Fusion Electronics/carrier files if implemented.
- [ ] Document PCBWay process/material/finish, released revision, quote/order, supplier feedback, received inspection, and design iterations.
- [ ] Publish the exact finished BOM and reconcile it to photos/schematic/build.
- [ ] Include service/assembly and end-of-line/manufacturing-scale reasoning without claiming unperformed production/certification.

## Code, documentation, and media

- [ ] Clean release passes tests, Pyright, firmware/App builds, archive/package/license/secret inspection, and a clean extraction check.
- [ ] Final article follows `FINAL_ARTICLE_OUTLINE.md` with complete build/use/troubleshooting/results/limitations.
- [ ] Final video prioritizes a continuous physical creator workflow and shows uncertainty/background plus recovery/privacy.
- [ ] All photos/video/audio/fonts/assets are original or permissioned and credited; participants/locations have consent.
- [ ] Source, App, schematic, CAD, and media links work logged out on desktop and phone.

## Rubric review

- [ ] Creativity 30: portable detached-pod interaction and uncertainty are obvious in the first minute/page.
- [ ] Documentation 20: a fresh reader can reproduce the build and distinguish evidence tiers.
- [ ] BOM 20: exact, complete, sourced, priced, reconciled to the build.
- [ ] Schematics 10: readable real connectivity/power/output design plus source.
- [ ] Fusion 10: native parametric assembly/electronics/drawings/DFM and iterations are substantive.
- [ ] Code 10: reusable, tested, pinned, documented, contribution-ready source is accessible.

## Account-controlled finish

- [ ] Every required platform field, Things item, category, attachment, image, and video is saved after refresh.
- [ ] Preview the complete public project logged out; inspect mobile formatting and downloads.
- [ ] Save final PDF/screenshots, rules snapshot, artifact hashes, URLs, submission timestamp, and confirmation.
- [ ] Submit before the correction buffer and reopen the project to verify submitted state.
